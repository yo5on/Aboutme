A 2D side-scrolling platformer built in Unity, and **playable right in the browser** on desktop and mobile.

- **Three levels** with checkpoints, health, level goals and dynamic audio
- **Six playable characters**, picked in a customization scene and saved between visits
- **Enemy AI:** goblins patrol, turn at ledges and slash when you're in reach; a boss guards until it spots you, then charges
- Touch joystick and jump button on phones; stomp enemies to defeat them

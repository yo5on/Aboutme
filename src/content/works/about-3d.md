The page you're on. **Scrolling is the camera move:** each résumé entry is a stop on a camera path around my 3D bust, with auto-focus depth of field, bloom and a turntable spin as the works slide in.

The nose bandage and the kiss on the cheek are decals projected onto the face mesh at load time, so they wrap the real geometry and move with it.

Layout and motion are adapted from the open-source [sen-3d-resume](https://github.com/dayinji/sen-3d-resume) (MIT).

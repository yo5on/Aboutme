A **four-degree-of-freedom** arm for pick-and-place, driven wirelessly from a FlySky RC transmitter.

The ESP32 turns receiver channels into servo motion so the **base, shoulder, elbow and gripper** each move independently. LEDs show system status, and push buttons add local control. The repo also includes the 3D-printable parts (STL).

An **art-magazine layout built entirely in 2D**: a pale engraved moth presented like a natural-history specimen, wrapped in crimson silk ribbons drawn in SVG.

Selected work is laid out as magazine spreads, each opening into a full-screen case view with its own link. Motion stays restrained and respects reduced-motion preferences.

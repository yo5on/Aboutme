A tiny character that lives on a **128×64 OLED** and picks its own faces based on how it feels.

- **40 expressions** built from 34 animations, morphed shape by shape, never cut
- **Moods** that build over time, and two buttons it remembers you pressing (tap, poke, or pester it until it gets cross)
- Falls **asleep** when left alone, breathes slowly, and wakes on its own
- Both buttons open **Gallery Mode** for photos and short videos, then hand the screen back to Sparky

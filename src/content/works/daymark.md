A study planner that **bends instead of breaking** when the day goes off-plan.

- **Quick Change:** plain-language requests like "I only have 60 minutes today" or "my DBMS exam is tomorrow", each shown for review before it's applied
- **Missed sessions** move to the next free slot, never overlapping
- **Confidence check-ins** after each session update mastery and queue low-confidence topics for revision
- Weekly planner, focus timer, streaks and insights. Runs in the browser and keeps data on the device

Planning is rule-based TypeScript, not a language model, so it's predictable, works offline and is covered by unit tests.

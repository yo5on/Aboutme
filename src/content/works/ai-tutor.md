A study workspace that **teaches concepts step by step** from your own material.

- **Tutor:** explains from the basics with examples and analogies, checks understanding, spots misconceptions, and gives hints before answers
- **Study materials:** upload PDF or TXT files and tutor or quiz from their text
- **Quizzes:** multiple choice from a topic or a document, checked on the server with explanations
- **Progress:** accuracy, weak topics, missed questions and study time

The Gemini key stays in server code: the browser only talks to the app's own API routes.
